# Code of Conduct

ORVYN follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and take feedback gracefully, and focus on what is
best for the people who use ORVYN. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others'
private information are not tolerated, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the project is represented.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through the contact link at
<https://orvyn.dubey.app/privacy>. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that don't follow this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful, following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant.
