# Vision

**ORVYN is a private operating system for one person's life** — and for the handful of people they choose to
share it with. It replaces the pile of separate apps (to-do list, calendar, expense tracker, notes, password
manager, file storage, chat) with one place that understands all of it.

## Who it is for

1. **The founder** — daily, on every device. If he does not open it every day, it has failed.
2. **Friends he invites (5–50)** — the same deployment, with only the features he enables for each of them.
3. Possibly later: a product. Nothing in the design should block that, but nothing is built for it yet.

## The four ideas that make it different

1. **Capture is effortless, or it does not happen.** Most personal apps die because entering data is work.
   ORVYN's default input is not a form. It is: say it, photograph it, forward it, share it, or let it be read
   automatically (bank SMS, email, calendar). Forms exist for editing, not for entry.
2. **One inbox for everything ("Drop").** Anything dropped in — a receipt photo, a voice note, a screenshot of
   a message, a PDF, a link — is understood and filed as the right thing: an expense, a task, an event, a
   document with its expiry date and a reminder. You confirm with one tap; you never choose a category first.
3. **One assistant that can act.** Not a chatbot bolted on: an assistant with the same abilities as the app.
   "What did I spend on food this month, and move the dentist to Friday" is one request. It always shows what
   it is about to change and waits for a yes on anything that matters.
4. **It comes to you.** A morning brief, a nudge before a document expires, a warning when spending drifts,
   an IPO closing tomorrow that fits your rules. Quiet by default; every notification must earn its place.

## Principles

- **Private first.** Encrypted everywhere; the vault is unreadable even to the server; the person can export
  or delete everything.
- **Fast and offline.** The phone works on a train with no signal and syncs later. Nothing waits on a spinner
  that could have been instant.
- **Beautiful on purpose.** The interface is a reason to open the app (see `design/principles.md`).
- **Modular.** Every feature is a module. Adding the next one must not require touching the others.
- **Honest AI.** It says where an answer came from, says when it does not know, and never moves money, sends
  messages or deletes things on its own.

## What it is not

- Not a social network, not a team workspace, not a bank. It tracks money; it never moves it.
- Not financial advice. The IPO analyst explains and scores; the decision is the person's.
