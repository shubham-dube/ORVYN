# Chat and Split — how to use them

## Chat

- Give a friend **Chat** in People. Their device makes its own keys the first time they open ORVYN; from then on,
  everything you send them is encrypted on your device for each of their devices.
- **Chat → New**: one person for a direct chat, several for a group (you are its admin: rename, add, remove).
- Hover a message on the web (long-press on the phone) to **react**, **reply**, **edit** or **delete for everyone**.
  The paperclip sends photos and files, encrypted before they leave your device.
- ✓ Sent, ✓✓ Read; "is typing…"; **mute** per chat in its details.
- Details (ⓘ) show each person's **device codes**. Compare them in person once to be sure nobody is in between.
- A new phone or browser can't read messages from before it joined — that is the price of the server never holding
  your keys.

## Split

1. **Split → New group**: a name ("Goa trip"), friends on ORVYN, and **guests** (just a name and phone). Optionally a
   group chat with it.
2. **Add expense**: what for, amount, who paid — one person or **several contributions** — and how it splits:
   **equally** (tick who shares it), **exact ₹**, **%** or **shares** (2 : 1 : 1). The preview shows each person's
   part before you save.
3. **Settle up** shows the fewest transfers that clear everyone. **Pay by UPI** opens your UPI app with the amount;
   **Record payment** when money changes hands; **Remind** pings friends, or opens WhatsApp for guests with their
   private link.
4. The wallet icon on an expense adds **your share to Money**. **Summary** copies the whole picture for any chat;
   **Share** sends the group as a card into a chat.
